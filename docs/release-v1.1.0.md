# Quiet Covers v1.1.0 · Letterforms & rhythm

The title and author now have a literary voice of their own. All twenty Chinese/English covers have been re-typeset while keeping the ten illustration originals unchanged.

- Chinese titles use Zhuque Fangsong, with selective Ma Shan Zheng brush lettering. Stepped lines, open short titles and upright vertical columns respond to each work.
- English titles pair Cormorant Garamond roman with true italic; author names receive a larger, deliberate setting.
- Per-language typography plans are editable in `catalog.json`. The renderer measures actual ink bounds, including italic overhangs, and preserves exact title and author wording.
- The standalone skill gains a focused typography guide and six-cover visual specimen.
- Five refreshed promotional sheets include a before/after comparison. Font sources, hashes and OFL notices are bundled with the source.
- Nine tests pass, covering all twenty layouts, vertical reading order, source-image integrity and export behavior.
