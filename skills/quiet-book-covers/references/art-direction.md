# Art direction / 视觉语言

The reference series feels like a small-press literary collection: warm paper, quiet colour, a clear visual relationship, and typography that can breathe. Its force comes from scale and absence.

## Four anchors

| Study | Image | What carries the meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary World / 平凡的世界 | `images/ordinary-world.jpg` | An ivory path moves through heavy ochre earth toward one small doorway. |
| How the Steel Was Tempered / 钢铁是怎样炼成的 | `images/steel-tempered.jpg` | Tracks converge into winter; unprinted paper becomes snow. |
| White Horse Neighs in the West Wind / 白马啸西风 | `images/white-horse.jpg` | A small horse within a wide dune lets distance speak. |
| The Count of Monte Cristo / 基督山伯爵 | `images/monte-cristo.jpg` | The sea both surrounds the prison and suggests freedom beyond it. |

Do not trace these images. Carry their decisions into a different scene.

## Material and composition

- Matte warm ivory near `#f1ede3`. Keep texture subtle enough for legible small titles.
- Broad printed forms, a few uneven contour lines, restrained contrast. Not glossy 3D, an intricate scenic painting, or a heavily distressed poster.
- Default palette families: ochre + ink blue; slate + copper; dusty rose + oxblood; stone + charcoal. Green is not part of the founding palette, but may be introduced if the new brief justifies it.
- Portrait 2:3 by default. A calm upper title field, a narrative lower field, and an author margin. The illustration may meet the side edges without becoming a framed vignette.
- A path, bank, threshold, shadow or horizon gives the eye somewhere to go. Do not add one mechanically if the scene already works.

## What failed in the early experiments

A large isolated dragon and a pair of crossed blades were readable but too much like emblems for this particular project. The lesson is not “never draw a dragon or sword.” It is to avoid choosing a noun before deciding what the viewer should feel. One compelling setting with an implied story can do more than a collection of accurate props.

## Typography

Treat the image as text-free artwork. Keep exact titles and author names in data. Use a serif with complete Chinese and Latin coverage; the reference implementation uses Noto Serif SC under OFL 1.1. A quiet sans-serif may be used for interface labels.

Title, translated subtitle and author are three distinct roles. Long titles should wrap or reduce size within a deliberate range. Never let a typesetting fix obscure a failed composition.
