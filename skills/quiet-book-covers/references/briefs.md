# Worked briefs

These are visual interpretations, not literary summaries or claims about a specific edition.

## 平凡的世界 / Ordinary World

**Tension:** a difficult daily life, with hope kept small and close.
**Scene:** two ochre curves of loess; one small dark cave doorway.
**Decision:** an ivory path climbs through the earth. Let the paper make the path.
**Omit:** portraits, multiple houses, machinery, montage.

## 钢铁是怎样炼成的 / How the Steel Was Tempered

**Tension:** endurance against cold.
**Scene:** railway tracks converge through slate-blue snowbanks.
**Decision:** a small copper glint at the vanishing point interrupts the winter.
**Omit:** slogans, flags, heroic poses, an industrial panorama.

## 白马啸西风 / White Horse Neighs in the West Wind

**Tension:** freedom and solitude coexist.
**Scene:** a small pale horse crossing one low ochre dune.
**Decision:** make the horse small enough that the desert matters.
**Omit:** rider, detailed anatomy, border, ornamental scenery.

## 基督山伯爵 / The Count of Monte Cristo

**Tension:** confinement and the possibility of escape.
**Scene:** an island fortress within dark blue water.
**Decision:** a single warm opening, broad water curves, generous paper sky.
**Omit:** chains, crowns, sword, ship, portrait.

## Beyond a literal title

**The Metamorphosis:** rather than an insect emblem, try a closed door and an oversized, unclaimed shadow within a familiar room. Estrangement comes from the space.

**Rickshaw Boy:** rather than a camel taken from the Chinese title, try a narrowing lane, high walls and a small vacant rickshaw. The lane gives the vehicle a human context.
